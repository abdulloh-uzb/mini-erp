# Loans stay out of net profit

The brief asks for net profit to "take loans into account". We keep loan principal (given, received, repaid) out of P&L entirely and show **Cash on hand** and **Loans outstanding** next to **Net profit** instead. Under NSBU 21 loans live on balance-sheet accounts (5830, 6810, 6820), and the Tax Code (art. 304) excludes borrowed money from income. MoySklad and Poster build P&L the same way. Only loan interest enters P&L. See `docs/research/uzbek-pos-erp-competitors.md`.
